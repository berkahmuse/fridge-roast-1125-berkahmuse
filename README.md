## The line

> My smart fridge updated itself at 3 a.m., locked the ice maker behind a subscription, and now judges my milk like a sommelier who peaked in high school.

152 characters. One line, roasting a smart fridge: its firmware update, its subscription-locked ice maker, and its opinions about the milk.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
